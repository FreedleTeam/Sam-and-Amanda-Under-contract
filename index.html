Your #1 action · Please schedule this week

Schedule the septic inspection & ownership-transfer permit process

Elbert County requires a septic inspection and a Use Permit for the transfer of a property served by an onsite wastewater treatment system. Under our contract, the seller is responsible for providing and paying for the required permit.

  1. Open the Elbert County onsite wastewater page and find the licensed septic inspectors/pumpers directory.
  2. Call a licensed inspector and request a transfer-of-title septic inspection for 27083 Pine Vista Circle, Kiowa.
  3. Ask whether the provider coordinates any needed pumping and submits the inspection report and Use Permit application to the county. Confirm the total cost and timing.
  4. Send me the company's name and appointment date once it's scheduled.

County contact: Elbert County Public Health · 720-595-3620 · publichealth@elbertcounty-co.gov. Confirm current county fees and turnaround times when scheduling.
